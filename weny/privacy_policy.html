Quiet Horizon Privacy Map

What stays on your device, and what briefly crosses the sky

Weny is an account-free sky studio. This map explains, in plain studio language, how photos, Sparks, preferences, and casting signals move while you create. It is written for curious makers who want clarity without a maze of formal jargon — a soft lantern for the path from Camera Roll to Vault.

No login profile On-device Vault Cast-time AI processing StoreKit Spark top-ups

Layer A · Identity without accounts

Weny does not ask you to register an email, phone number, or social profile to enter Studio, Gallery, Vault, or Me. There is no Weny username hanging over your creations. Continuity comes from local state on the device you are holding: Spark balance, unlocked rare skies, default blend, haptic preference, and the keepsakes you choose to save.

Opening Weny never requires a password wall. Your creative history lives as local Vault entries, not as a cloud social graph.

Layer B · Photos you choose to cast

Sky casting begins only when you pick an image from the library or capture one with the camera. System permission sheets appear for Photo Library, saving to Photos, camera, and related device prompts when those features are used. Weny does not browse your entire camera roll in the background.

  • Selected image bytes are used to identify the sky region and generate a replacement atmosphere.
  • Before an image is sent for AI processing, Weny shows a confirmation so the casting step stays intentional.
  • After generation, you decide whether the result becomes a Vault keepsake, a Photos export, or a temporary preview you leave behind.
Best-fit frames Outdoor scenes with a clear horizon help the sky mask feel natural and reduce awkward edges.
Your call on originals Keep Original Photo stores an unedited companion file beside each keepsake when that preference is on.

Layer C · On-device memory

Much of Weny prefers to stay home on your device. Typical on-device pieces include:

  • Spark balance and a readable Spark ledger of credits and casts.
  • Which rare skies you have permanently unlocked.
  • Creation Preferences such as auto light matching, default blend, keep-original, and haptics.
  • Vault image files and optional original companions under the app documents area.
  • Onboarding completion and first-use AI data disclosure acknowledgment flags.

Removing a keepsake from Vault deletes its local files and list entry. Resetting or deleting the app removes this local observatory unless the system still holds exported Photos you saved separately.

Layer D · The brief journey of a cast

When you confirm a cast, Weny builds an editing instruction from the chosen sky style, blend strength, and lighting preference, then sends the image for AI sky recognition and repainting. The goal is a coherent new sky with color temperature and rim light that feel at home with your subject. The returned frame is shown in the Sky Renewed stage where you can compare, save to Vault, or export.

Blend tiers whisper different moods: subtle and natural, balanced and true, or boldly dramatic. Auto light matching on uses the sky’s lighting note; off asks to keep original lighting character. Processing exists to complete your creative request, not to build a public identity dossier.

Layer E · Sparks and storefront signals

Spark top-ups use Apple’s StoreKit purchase flow. Product titles, localized prices, and purchase sheets are presented by the App Store. Weny records successful consumable deliveries as Spark credits in your local balance and ledger. The bazaar may also show restore-friendly messaging when purchases are unavailable.

  • Weny does not ask you to type card numbers into the app itself.
  • Purchase confirmations and receipts follow Apple’s storefront experience.
  • Spark history lines stay readable inside the in-app ledger for your own recall.

Layer F · Tracking prompt, softly explained

On supported systems, Weny may show the App Tracking Transparency prompt so the platform can measure anonymous campaign performance and improve how people discover the app. Photos, Vault creations, and AI casting requests are not treated as advertising profiles. Your answer to the prompt is handled by the system settings you already know.

Layer G · Permissions you can see

Photo Library Select a frame to transform and, when you choose, save finished skies back into Photos.
Camera Capture a fresh outdoor moment and walk it straight into the casting ritual.
Add-only Photos Used when you tap Save to Photos so the finished piece can land in your library.
Haptics preference A local switch — when quiet, key taps stay silent even if the device supports vibration.

Layer H · What we aim not to do

  • We do not require an account wall before you create.
  • We do not turn your Vault into a public gallery without your export choice.
  • We do not need microphone access for core sky casting; any future audio note feature would ask clearly first.
  • We do not hide the casting confirmation that precedes AI image processing.

If something in the flow ever feels unclear, pause at the confirmation sheet, review Creation Preferences, or write to the studio desk before casting a sensitive frame.

Layer I · Children and careful makers

Weny is crafted as a creative camera companion for general audiences who understand photo permissions and in-app Spark purchases. Guardians who share a device with younger makers should keep App Store purchase settings and Photo access aligned with their household comfort. The studio experience stays local-first so family devices can remain predictable.

Layer J · Talking with the studio

Layer K · A quiet closing

Your photos are moments you lived. Weny borrows them only long enough to paint a new sky when you ask, then returns the finished frame to your hands — Vault, Photos, or a simple dismiss. Keep the preferences that feel right. Keep the rare skies you unlock. And know that the front door still opens without an account, every time the aurora inside the app lights up.
