2. Application Feature Overview

Weny provides a comprehensive toolkit for creative sky‑replacement photo editing. Our core feature‑set includes the ability to select source photographs directly from your device image library or capture new shots using your built‑in camera. You can browse our curated sky‑effect gallery to discover inspirational looks, preview different atmospheric styles, then manually fine‑tune image‑blend intensity, lighting levels, and visual opacity to achieve a seamless, natural‑looking final photograph.

Our interactive before‑and‑after draggable comparison slider allows instant side‑by‑side review between your unmodified original photo and your newly AI‑transformed version. Completed artwork can be safely saved inside your private, local‑only Vault storage space, or exported directly to your device’s system photo gallery. Additional configurable settings cover lighting adjustments, blend‑mode controls, and haptic feedback preferences to customize your editing workflow.

Before any selected photograph is transmitted to our AI creation service for processing, Weny will always request your explicit approval. No image upload or AI‑rendering workflow will begin without your active, intentional consent on the first time you trigger this feature. At any moment during an active AI generation task, you retain full permission to cancel, pause, or stop the ongoing photo‑transformation process, and no further data will be sent to our AI server once you terminate the operation.

4. User Data & Image Handling Guidelines

Protecting your creative content and personal privacy is one of our highest priorities while you use Weny. This section describes our data‑handling practices for photo files and device‑related information:

  • Weny will never collect, store, or track your real‑time geographic or device location data at any point during app usage.
  • Weny does not scan, extract, collect, or save facial recognition, face‑mapping, or biometric facial data from any of your uploaded or edited photographs.
  • Original source photographs remain stored locally on your own device. Your private Vault gallery is saved to local storage only and is not automatically synced to remote cloud servers.
  • Photos you choose to submit for AI sky‑effect generation are only temporarily transmitted to our AI processing endpoint for the single purpose of rendering your requested sky visual effect. Once image processing completes, we do not retain copies of your submitted source photos on our AI service servers.
