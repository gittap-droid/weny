01

The open door

Weny is built so you can walk in instantly. You do not create a password, join a waiting list, or bind a profile before casting a sky. Your session lives on your device through local preferences, Spark balances, unlocked rare skies, and creations you choose to keep. When you reopen the app, your Studio remembers where you left the blend slider, whether haptic feedback hums softly, and which rare horizons already belong to you.

Think of Weny as a pocket observatory: the telescope is ready the moment you lift it.
02

What Weny is for

Weny focuses on outdoor photographs with a readable horizon. The ritual is simple and intentional:

  • Upload or capture a portrait or landscape where the sky still has room to breathe.
  • Let the AI identify the sky region and prepare a natural transition into a new atmosphere.
  • Choose a sky style from clear daylight to Rose Twilight, Aurora Veil, Meteor Rain, and more.
  • Tune the blend so the result feels subtle, balanced, or boldly dramatic.
  • Save the creation into your Vault, export to Photos, or cast another sky.

03

Sparks, the soft currency of casting

Sparks power creations and permanent unlocks for rare skies. A new light starter pack greets first arrivals so you can explore immediately. Later, you may top up Sparks through the in-app Spark Bazaar. Prices shown in the bazaar follow what the App Store presents for each bundle. When a purchase completes, Sparks appear in your balance and a short line is written into your Spark ledger so you can revisit the story of every credit and cast.

Rare skies ask for a one-time Spark unlock. After that unlock, the sky stays with you for unlimited future creations. Common skies remain open without unlock rituals.

04

Your Vault and your originals

When a creation is kept, Weny stores the forged image locally in your Vault. If Keep Original Photo is enabled in Creation Preferences, the unedited companion file sits beside it so you can compare before and after. You may open any keepsake, save it to the system Photos library, or discard it. Discarding removes the local files and the Vault record together.

The Vault is your private sky diary on this device, not a public feed.
05

Photos, camera, and the casting path

To replace a sky, Weny needs a photo from your library or camera. Permission prompts appear only when those paths are used. During casting, the selected image may be sent for AI sky recognition and repainting so the new atmosphere can be generated. You confirm this step before the image travels for processing. After the result returns, you decide whether it becomes a Vault keepsake or remains a momentary experiment.

  • Clear horizons and clean skies usually produce the most natural blends.
  • Auto light matching asks the AI to harmonize subject lighting with the chosen sky mood.
  • Turning that preference off asks the process to preserve the original lighting character more closely.
06

A gentle code of the studio

Use Weny for personal creative expression, portfolio mood boards, travel memories, and playful sky experiments. Choose photos you are comfortable editing. Respect the people who appear in your frames. Avoid using the casting tools to invent scenes that could confuse someone about real events when that clarity matters. Weny thrives as a imaginative craft space, not a stage for harmful or misleading spectacle.

If a sky style, prompt fragment, or generated frame feels off, try another sky, adjust blend, or start a fresh cast. The Studio is designed for iteration.

07

Preferences that shape the ritual

Inside Creation Preferences you can:

  • Toggle auto light matching for future casts.
  • Set the default blend strength that greets every new blend stage.
  • Decide whether originals are stored beside keepsakes.
  • Enable or quiet haptic feedback on key interactions.

These choices stay on device and apply the next time you open the casting flow. They are yours to revise whenever the mood of your practice changes.
